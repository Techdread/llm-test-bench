DEFENSE TACTICS

Towers & Capabilities

• Gatling: Rapid single-target physical bullets.
• Plasma: Continuous thermal energy beam that melts armor.
• Mortar: High splash area explosive damage with slow rate.
• Cryo: Emitters pulse freeze aura, slowing enemy movement speed.
• Tesla: Arc electricity jumps dynamically between nearby targets.

Upgrade & Targeting Strategy

Click any placed tower to open its inspector panel. Upgrade damage, range, and fire rate up to Level 4. You can also customize target priority (First, Last, Strongest, Weakest, Closest).

Controls Summary

• Mouse Drag: Orbit Camera | Scroll: Zoom
• Keys [1-5]: Select Tower Type | [Esc]: Cancel/Deselect
• Key [Space]: Toggle Pause/Resume | [C]: Cycle Camera Angles
