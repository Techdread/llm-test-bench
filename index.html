Your data, your models

A local-first website

The site is hosted, but your working files are not uploaded to us. In Chrome or Edge, choose a folder on your own computer. The apps read and write inside that folder with your permission.

  1. Choose a folderThe browser remembers the handle and asks before restoring access.
  2. Connect a modelUse your local LM Studio/OpenAI-compatible server or your own OpenRouter key.
  3. Generate and comparePrompts go only to the provider you select; saved outputs remain in your folder.
Best in Chrome or Edge. Folder access, local-network permission, and screen recording depend on browser APIs that are not equally available everywhere. Local model servers work directly from the hosted site; CLI coding agents and cross-app handoffs belong to the full local DevTools Hub.
